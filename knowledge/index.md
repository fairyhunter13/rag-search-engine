# Subdirectories

* [constraints](constraints/index.md) - What the retriever and its host fix: pooling, language
  discovery, and the caller's own workspace as the search unit. Also a throttled card that cannot
  produce an admissible latency number.
* [decisions](decisions/index.md) - Rulings on chunking, embedding, storage and surface, each stated with the measurement that would reverse it.
* [defects](defects/index.md) - Failures in indexing, deletion and shutdown, most of them cases where a green answer and an empty one were indistinguishable.
* [runbooks](runbooks/index.md) - Three procedures for the GPU host: restoring Dynamic Boost, restoring the registry, and running anything that touches the real card.
